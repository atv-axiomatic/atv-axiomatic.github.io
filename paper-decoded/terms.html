Connecting TikTok and YouTube

The app connects to an account only when its owner signs in on TikTok's or Google's own sign-in page and approves it. It uses each connection only for what the owner asks it to do:

AccountPermissionWhat the app does with it
TikTokuser.info.basicShows which account is connected (its display name)
TikTokvideo.uploadUploads a finished video to the owner's TikTok inbox, where the owner reviews it and posts it in the TikTok app
TikTokvideo.publish (only if enabled)Posts the owner's video directly, with the privacy level the owner picks
YouTubeyoutube.uploadUploads the owner's video as a Short, with the title, description and privacy the owner sets
YouTubeyoutube.readonlyShows which channel is connected (its name)

The owner can disconnect an account at any time in the app's Settings, and can also revoke access from TikTok's or Google's account settings.
