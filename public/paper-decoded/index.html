Paper Decoded Studio

Research papers, decoded in about a minute.

Paper Decoded makes short vertical videos that explain one research paper at a time. Paper Decoded Studio is the private desktop tool we make them with: it writes and renders each video, then publishes it to Paper Decoded's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What the app does with TikTok and YouTube

When the owner of the Paper Decoded accounts connects them, the app uploads a finished video to the owner's TikTok inbox (where the owner reviews and posts it in the TikTok app), or to YouTube as a Short with its title and description. It only posts the owner's own videos, to the owner's own accounts, and only when the owner asks. It is not offered to the public.
