Paper Decoded Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

In short

Paper Decoded Studio ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of Paper Decoded's TikTok and YouTube accounts, to publish the short explainer videos Paper Decoded makes. It keeps the owner's sign-in tokens on the owner's own computer, uses them only to upload the owner's videos, and collects no data about anyone else.

What the app receives

The app receives this only when the owner connects an account on TikTok's or Google's own sign-in page:

WhatFromWhy
Account ID (open_id) and display nameTikTokTo show which TikTok account is connected
Access and refresh tokensTikTokTo upload the owner's videos without signing in each time
Channel ID and channel nameYouTube (Google)To show which channel is connected
Refresh tokenYouTube (Google)To upload the owner's videos without signing in each time
The videos, titles and descriptions the owner chooses to publishThe owner's computerTo send them to TikTok or YouTube when the owner asks

The app also stores the owner's own developer keys for the TikTok and Google apps, which the owner enters in its Settings.

How it is used

The app uses this information for two things only: showing the owner which accounts are connected, and uploading a video when the owner clicks to publish it. It does not use it for anything else.

Where it is kept and for how long

Everything above is kept in one file on the owner's computer that only the owner's user account can read. The app has no server and no cloud storage of its own. The tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account in the app's Settings, which deletes them, or deletes the app and that file.

Sharing

Paper Decoded does not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. The app sends it only to TikTok or Google, and only to upload a video the owner chose to publish. What happens to a video once it reaches them is covered by TikTok's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

What the app never does

  • Through the connected accounts, it never reads or stores data about other TikTok or YouTube users, such as followers, comments or messages.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • It never posts anything the owner did not choose to publish.

Your choices

  • Disconnect an account in the app's Settings: its tokens are deleted at once.
  • Revoke access from the platform's side: in TikTok's settings under security and permissions, or in your Google Account under third-party connections.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app and its data file from the computer.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone but its adult owner.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it. Questions about privacy: email Paper Decoded at ab.tech.ventures@gmail.com.
